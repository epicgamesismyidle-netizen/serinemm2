SerineMM – Telegram Escrow Bot

SerineMM is a neutral Telegram escrow bot designed to facilitate secure cryptocurrency transactions between two parties. The bot acts as a procedural middleman, ensuring funds are only released when agreed conditions are fulfilled.

Add SerineMM to Telegram Group

How It Works

  1. Add SerineMM to your Telegram group.
  2. Use the /open_ticket command to start a deal.
  3. The buyer deposits funds into escrow.
  4. The seller delivers the agreed goods or services.
  5. Funds are released once both parties confirm completion.

Bot Commands

  • /start — Initializes the bot and displays information.
  • /open_ticket — Opens a new escrow ticket for a deal.

Trust & Transparency

SerineMM operates with neutrality and transparency. Funds are held strictly according to predefined escrow rules and are never released arbitrarily. All actions occur within Telegram and are visible to the involved parties.

SerineMM does not provide financial, legal, or investment advice. Users are responsible for performing their own due diligence before engaging in transactions.
